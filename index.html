It's been kind of a rough year for us two, but somehow, we managed to scale through the hard times, even those moments when we didn't know what we felt towards each other or where we stood. We've had to learn each other's differences, adjust in certain ways, unlearn some things, and understand that sometimes, loving each other means seeing beyond our individual perspectives. Looking back, I know there are moments I could have handled better, things I could have said differently, and ways I could have shown up for you better. I don't know what the future holds for us, but I know I'm grateful we got to experience this year together. Thank you for the laughter, the memories, the affection, the lessons, and even the moments that challenged us to see things differently. Thank you for being someone whose presence I've come to crave and whose place in my life has meant so much to me. Here's to the memories we've made, the things we've learned about each other, and the moments that made this year ours. Here's to being my wife, to the love we've shared, and to the fact that, in spite of everything, you remain someone so special to me. Happy anniversary, bunny bear. ❤️
